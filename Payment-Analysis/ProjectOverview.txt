Payment Operations Analysis Project
1. Problem Statement
The banking department faced significant challenges in payment processing:

- Inconsistent transaction accuracy across different systems
- Lack of visibility into operational inefficiencies
- No standardized method to track and improve payment processing performance
- Difficulty in making strategic decisions about operational migrations
- Suboptimal resource allocation and employee scheduling

2. Solution and Business Value
Our data-driven approach transformed payment operations by:

- Identifying root causes of processing errors
- Implementing a 4-eyes principle for high-risk transactions and most common errors (done)
- Developing targeted employee training programs (done)
- Achieving 95-99% transaction accuracy(done)
- Enabling data-backed decisions for:transition monitoring and transition of the payments

Resource allocation - hiring more employees (done)
Employee scheduling - leaves planning (done)
Operational efficiency improvements (done) from 50-60% accuracy to 95-99% accuracy)

Key Quantifiable Outcomes:

Reduced processing errors by up to 50%
Increased operational capacity without additional headcount
Provided predictive insights for operational expansions
Enabled more strategic workforce management
